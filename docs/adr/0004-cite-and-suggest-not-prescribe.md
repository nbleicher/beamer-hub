# AI may Cite and Suggest, not prescribe

Check Sequences and later Shopping may use a model to pull Citations and rank Suggestions. The app will not issue a work order, name a failed part as fact, or tell the Owner to buy a specific SKU.

Prescribe was rejected: on this E46 a confident “replace the window regulator” is the common wrong answer. Cite + Suggest keeps the Owner as the person who decides.
