# Reminders match System, with these defaults

Each Service Record has one System. A Reminder fires from the last Record of that System. Recommended intervals the Owner can override: engine-oil 5,000 mi or 12 months; brake-fluid 24 months; coolant 48 months; cabin-filter 12 months.

Matching on Region or Visit was rejected: one Gatto’s day is oil and coils; only oil should move the oil Reminder.
