# Catalog source is an Owner-supplied VIN ETK dump

The Catalog is filled from an ETK export the Owner obtains for VIN `WBAET374X3NJ32467` (serial `J32467`). Until that dump arrives, the Catalog may be empty; the Owner may still paste one BMW part number at a time. We will not scrape RealOEM, etkbmw.cc, BMW NA, AIR, or ETK, and we will not ask a model to invent Parts.

There is no public VIN-true parts API. Dealer/AOS licensing is not a dump we can take. The Owner bringing their own print or export is the legitimate bulk path.
